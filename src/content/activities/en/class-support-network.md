---
title: "Classroom Support Network"
format: "Work in groups of 4–5 people"
time: "15–20 minutes"
goal: "Recognizing sources of support, strengthening peer solidarity and developing awareness of the school climate."
order: 14
---

## Preparation

Place 2–3 navy blue cards representing difficult situations in the center of a large sheet of paper.

## Instructions

1. Groups think about ways to get support, such as *“What helps you feel safe?”*
2. Around the cards, students write down specific people, places, and ways of support they can use at school, e.g. a counselor or school psychologist, friends, a quiet place, deep breathing.
3. Students then draw lines connecting specific support methods to challenging situations. The lines can represent “ropes” that create a visual network of safety and support.

## Variants

- **Option 1 – Test-time kit:** Focus on ways to deal with test anxiety and study-related stress.
