---
title: "Empathy Bridge"
format: "Work in pairs"
time: "10–15 minutes"
goal: "Showing understanding in difficult situations and developing supportive communication."
order: 10
---

## Preparation

Each pair chooses 1 navy blue card representing a difficult emotion (anger, despair, sadness, etc.).

## Instructions

1. Students briefly think about what would make the character feel heard and understood.
2. The pair writes a short, supportive message to the character depicted on the card.

## Variants

- **Variant – Reversing the situation:** Students also choose a card representing a solution to a problem, peace, or a situation that improves well-being and consider what actions might help to move from one state to the other.
