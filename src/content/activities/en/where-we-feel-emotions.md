## Instructions

1. Display a card representing a chosen emotion. Ask students to consider what sensations might occur in their bodies when experiencing that emotion.
2. Say: *“Think about where in your body you might feel this emotion. You can point to that spot on your body.”*
3. Ask students to point to selected areas, such as the stomach, heart, hands, face, or arms.

> 💡 **Tip:** Emphasize that everyone can experience emotions differently.
