---
title: "What Could Have Happened?"
format: "Group work / whole class"
time: "7 minutes"
order: 20
---

## Instructions

1. Display a card depicting a person experiencing a specific emotion.
2. Divide students into small groups and say: *“Think about what might have happened before to make this person feel this way.”*
3. After a few minutes, ask the groups to present their ideas.

> 💡 **Tip:** Emphasize that we don’t know the true story of the card’s protagonist, and the answers presented are only possible explanations.
