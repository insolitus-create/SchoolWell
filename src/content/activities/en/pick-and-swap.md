---
title: "Select and Replace"
format: "Physical activity / pair work"
time: "5–7 minutes"
goal: "Building relationships with peers, short conversations and casual interaction."
order: 3
---

## Preparation

Give each student one navy blue card.

## Instructions

1. Students move around the room.
2. When the teacher claps or says STOP, students pair up with the person closest to them.
3. Each person says in one sentence what emotion they see on their card and what caught their attention.
4. The students then swap cards and begin moving again.
5. Repeat the exercise for 2–3 rounds.

## Variants

- **Option 1 – Swap questions:** Instead of using navy blue cards, give half the class green cards. Those with green cards pair up with those with navy blue cards and ask them the questions from the green cards.
