---
title: "Emotion Comic"
format: "Work in small groups (3–4 people)"
time: "15–20 minutes"
goal: "Developing cause-and-effect thinking, understanding the sequence of events and the relationship between situations and emotions."
order: 13
---

## Preparation

Each group draws 3 navy blue cards.

## Instructions

1. Groups place 3 cards next to each other.
2. They then create a story explaining what caused the change from the situation depicted on card 1 to the situation on card 3.
3. Each group presents their three-stage story to the class.

## Variants

- **Option 1 – The missing middle:** The teacher shows the group card 1 and card 3. Students invent and write down or draw a missing event that could have led from the first situation to the second.
- **Option 2 – The grand class chain:** Each group adds one card to the class story they’re creating together. Additional cards are added to the board, creating an increasingly longer story.
