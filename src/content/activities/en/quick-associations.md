---
title: "Instant Association"
format: "Circle work"
time: "5 minutes"
goal: "Naming emotions, spontaneous associations and group activation."
order: 2
---

## Preparation

Place a deck of navy blue cards face down in the center of the circle.

## Instructions

1. Turn over one navy blue card so everyone can see it.
2. Students take turns, clockwise, saying one word that comes to mind.
3. Previously given words cannot be repeated; if necessary, the student can skip their turn.
4. Repeat the exercise with 2–3 cards.

## Variants

- **Option 1 – Sound effect:** Students make a sound or sigh that matches the situation or emotion depicted on the card.
- **Option 2 – Color association:** Students name a color that they associate with the emotion shown on the card.
