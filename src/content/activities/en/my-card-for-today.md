## Instructions

1. Display several cards representing different emotions.
2. Say: *“Choose the card that best represents how you feel today.”*
3. Ask students to point to the card they have chosen.
4. Ask students who are willing to share why they chose a particular card.
5. Conclude by saying: *“Each of us may feel something different today. All emotions are important, and we can talk about them.”*

> 💡 **Tip:** This activity can be a starting point for students to create (draw / paint) their own card.
