---
title: "Without Words"
format: "Whole group"
time: "10–15 minutes"
goal: "Recognizing and expressing emotions through facial expressions, gestures and movement, and activating the group."
order: 7
---

## Preparation

Prepare the navy blue cards and choose a place where one student can stand facing the group.

## Instructions

1. One student draws a navy blue card and does not show it to the others.
2. The student looks at the illustration and imitates the emotion or situation depicted in it, using facial expressions, gestures and movement.
3. The other students observe and try to guess what emotion or situation is on the card.
4. The first person to guess correctly may become the next participant.
5. Repeat the game with a few more cards.

## Variants

- **Variant – Teams:** Divide the class into two teams. The first team to guess the emotion receives a point.
