---
title: "Emotion Thermometer"
format: "Whole class"
time: "3–5 minutes"
goal: "Quickly check the mood of the group, develop self-awareness."
order: 1
---

## Preparation

Place 4–5 navy blue cards representing different emotions on the board or spread them out on the floor.

## Instructions

1. Ask students: *“Look at these images for a moment. Which one best fits your mood right now?”*
2. Students can point to a selected card, stand next to it, or write the card number on a sticky note.
3. Ask 2–3 volunteer students: *“What word best describes this card?”*

## Variants

- **Option 1 – Morning and afternoon:** Do the exercise at the beginning and end of the day to observe changes in mood.
- **Option 2 – Finger evaluation:** Students point to the selected card and show 1 to 5 fingers, indicating the intensity of their mood.
