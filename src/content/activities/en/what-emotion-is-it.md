## Instructions

1. Display the chosen emotion card in the SchoolWell app.
2. Ask students to look carefully at the illustration.
3. Ask:
   - *“What do you think the person depicted on the card is feeling?”*
   - *“How can we recognize this emotion?”*
   - *“What on their face or in their posture indicates this emotion?”*

> 💡 **Tip:** Emphasize that the same illustration can be interpreted differently.
