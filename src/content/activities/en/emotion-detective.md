---
title: "Emotion Detective"
format: "Work in pairs"
time: "10–15 minutes"
goal: "Developing active listening, reading non-verbal signals and taking the other person’s perspective."
order: 9
---

## Preparation

Each pair receives 3 navy blue cards placed face down.

## Instructions

1. Student A looks at one card without showing it to student B.
2. Student A uses only body posture and facial expressions to convey the emotion or meaning of the card.
3. Student B tries to guess the emotion or situation depicted on the card.
4. Student A shows the card to check the answer.
5. The students then switch roles and continue playing with the remaining cards.

## Variants

- **Variant – Green card hint:** Student A draws a green card and answers the question on it. The answer serves as a hint for student B.
