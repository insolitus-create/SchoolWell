## Instructions

1. Display a card representing a chosen emotion. Divide students into pairs.
2. One child in a pair shows an emotion through facial expressions and body posture.
3. The second child imitates their movements like a reflection in a mirror.
4. After about a minute, ask students to switch roles.
5. Finally, ask: *“How can we tell what the other person is feeling?”*
