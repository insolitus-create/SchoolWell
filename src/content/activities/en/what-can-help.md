## Instructions

1. Display a card depicting a person experiencing a difficult emotion.
2. Ask: *“What would help this person feel better?”*
3. Write students’ suggestions on the board.

> 💡 **Tip:** Encourage them to suggest different ways to cope with emotions, such as talking to a loved one, resting, breathing calmly, playing, moving, or drawing.
