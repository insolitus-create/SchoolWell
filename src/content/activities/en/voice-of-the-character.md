---
title: "The Hero’s Voice"
format: "Pair work"
time: "10 minutes"
goal: "Developing empathy, the ability to express thoughts and feelings and take the perspective of another person."
order: 8
---

## Preparation

Navy blue cards. Students sit in pairs or in a circle.

## Instructions

1. Students choose one character from the illustration.
2. Say: *“Imagine you are this character. Complete the following sentences:”*
   - *“I am the character on this card, and I would like to say that…”*
   - *“Right now I feel…”*
   - *“I need…”*
3. If students are having difficulty, encourage them with additional questions: *“What would this character like to hear?”*

## Variants

- **Option 1 – Interview with a character:** One student takes on the role of a character and the other person acts as a reporter and asks questions such as *“What happened?”* and *“Who can help you?”*
- **Option 2 – Hero letter:** The student writes a short, 3-sentence message from the perspective of a chosen character.
- **Option 3 – What happens next?:** Students imagine what will happen to the hero 5 minutes later and tell the rest of their story.
