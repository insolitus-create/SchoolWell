---
title: "Where Am I Today?"
format: "Whole group / individual reflection"
time: "3–5 minutes"
goal: "Recognizing your own well-being, developing emotional self-awareness and starting classes calmly."
order: 4
---

## Preparation

Place several navy blue cards in different places around the room.

## Instructions

1. Lay out 5–6 navy blue cards and ask students to look at all the pictures for a moment.
2. Say: *“Choose the card that best represents how you feel today or what you bring to class.”*
3. Students approach the selected card.
4. Those interested can say one sentence: *“I chose this card because…”*
5. Emphasize that there are no right or wrong choices and everyone can decide whether they want to share their reflections.

## Variants

- **Option 1 – One word:** Each participant says only one word describing their current mood.
- **Option 2 – Without speaking:** Students simply choose a card without having to explain their choice.
